## Data Risk Summary

MedDefense's most significant data protection weakness is the exposure of **Restricted clinical data while in transit and in use across a flat, poorly monitored internal network**. Patient records, imaging data, medication data and billing information are accessed from many endpoints, but the environment lacks MFA, segmentation, centralized alerting and strong endpoint coverage on servers and mobile devices. The highest-risk gap is not one database alone; it is the combination of highly sensitive healthcare data, broad internal reachability, unmanaged or legacy devices and weak monitoring, meaning unauthorized access or modification may occur before MedDefense detects it.
